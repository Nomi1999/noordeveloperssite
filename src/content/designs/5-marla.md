A two-storey family home for a narrow plot, attached to its neighbours on both sides. On the ground floor, a drawing room sits at the front beside the car porch, with a TV lounge behind it, a kitchen opening onto a small rear yard, and a bedroom with its own bathroom.

Upstairs, the master bedroom has its own bathroom and a balcony behind a metal jaali. A second bedroom with a bathroom, a family lounge lit by an open sky court, and a laundry complete the floor. Outside: a covered car porch, planting along the front, and a boundary wall with a timber gate.
