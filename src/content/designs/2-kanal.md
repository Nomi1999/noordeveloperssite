A large two-storey family home built around a double-height lounge. The ground floor has a drawing room and dining for guests, the lounge and kitchen for the family, a back kitchen, and two bedrooms, each with its own bathroom.

On the first floor, a gallery looks down into the lounge. The master bedroom and four more bedrooms each have their own bathroom, most with a dressing, around a family lounge and a terrace. Outside: a covered porch, a long lawn along one side, a guard room by the gate, and a servant room at the back.
