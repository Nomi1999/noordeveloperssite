## Monogram (original)
Bounds x 421.5–593.5, y 346–511.5 (centered 7.5 units right of the axis). Parts: building-bar cluster
(two bars with a slot and a solid block, plus a roof ledge on the left), N stem with arch and base slab,
N diagonal ribbon that wraps into the D's inner bowl and stem, outer ribbon that sweeps under and up around
the D. Stroke width about 10.8 units throughout.
